Amira

From:cairo

location: Zamalek

food delivery to: Zamalek , Maadi , Nasr City , Giza , Abbassia .

Amira offers the most delicious traditional Egyptian dishes.

Featured Dishes :

Hawawshi:
Hawawshi is a crispy, pita stuffed Egyptian street food filled with spiced ground meat, onions, and herbs.

stuffed Grape leaves:
Stuffed grape leaves are filled with rice, herbs, spices, and sometimes meat, then simmered for flavor.

koshary:
Koshary is a flavorful Egyptian dish with rice, lentils, pasta, crispy onions, and spicy tomato sauce.

Molokhia:
Molokhia is a garlicky, aromatic soup made with jute leaves, broth, coriander, and lemon for rich flavor.

Fatta:
Fatta is a hearty dish with crispy bread, rice, meat, and garlicky broth, topped with vinegar sauce.

Mombar:
Mombar is stuffed intestines filled with rice, meat, onions, and spices, then boiled or fried.

Kunafa:
Kunafa is a crispy, golden dessert filled with gooey cream or cheese, drenched in fragrant sugar syrup.

Basbousa:
Basbousa is a semolina cake sweetened with syrup, baked, and topped with almonds or nuts for richness.

Om Ali:
Om Ali is a warm, baked dessert with pastry, milk, sugar, nuts, and dried fruits, golden and bubbly.

Rice Pudding:
Rice pudding is a creamy dessert with cooked rice, milk, sugar, and sometimes cream or egg yolks for richness.

Balah El Sham:
This crispy, golden treat is deep-fried, soaked in hot sugar syrup, and has a sweet, syrupy interior.

Mahalabia:
Mahalabia is a creamy dessert made with milk, sugar, and cornstarch, flavored with floral essences, and served chilled.
